About karate

Karate means "empty hand". It is a martial art that uses punches, kicks, blocks and stances, with no weapon needed.

Students learn kata (fixed forms), kumite (sparring) and basic techniques, and earn belts from white up to black as they improve.

  • Fitness Strength, speed, balance and flexibility.
  • Focus Better concentration at school and work.
  • Self-defence Practical skills and awareness.
  • Confidence Respect, courage and discipline.
